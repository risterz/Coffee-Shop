About Us

The story behind Bean Haven

Our Story

Started by siblings Anna and Mark, Bean Haven is built on a love for coffee and sustainability. What began as a shared dream during Sunday family dinners has evolved into the cozy space you see today.

After years in the corporate world, we decided to pursue our passion for creating a community space where people could enjoy exceptional coffee in a warm, welcoming environment.

Since opening our doors in 2020, we've been blessed to become a neighborhood favorite, a meeting place for friends, a workspace for creatives, and a second home to many of our regular customers.

Our Values

Sustainable Sourcing

We partner directly with small coffee farms to ensure fair prices and environmentally responsible farming practices. Every bean has a story, and we're proud to share it.

Community Connection

Bean Haven is more than a café—it's a community hub. We host events, support local artists, and actively participate in neighborhood initiatives.

Quality First

From our carefully selected beans to our house-made syrups, we never compromise on quality. If it's not excellent, it's not on our menu.
